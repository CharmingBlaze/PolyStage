PolyStage modeling icons in this folder are original 16x16 glyphs
drawn for this application. They are not copies of Blender icons.

License: MIT, same as the PolyStage application source.
Filled white (#fff) so CSS masks can tint them with currentColor.

Includes: move, rotate, scale, transform, pivot, orientation-local,
orientation-global, snap, center, extrude, inset, bevel, knife, loopcut,
cad, object, vertex, edge, face, camera, light, zoom, maximize, bone,
pose, weld, trash, outliner, add, mirror.

Blender-derived icons remain in /public/blender-icons (GPL-2.0+).
